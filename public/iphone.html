Terms and conditions

The fine print.

Read the full terms and conditions
  1. Organiser. This scheme is run by Raman Durga Das Seth ("RDDS"), 33 Amstel Mall, Mall Road, Amritsar, Punjab.
  2. Period. Purchases made from 1 November 2026 to 28 November 2026, 8:00 PM IST, qualify.
  3. Who can take part. Customers aged 18 and above who can collect the prize in person in Amritsar. RDDS staff and their immediate families cannot take part.
  4. Purchase tickets. One ticket for every full ₹25,000 of the final invoice value of new jewellery on a single bill. The minimum qualifying bill is ₹25,000. There is no upper limit on tickets. Bullion bars, coins and repairs do not qualify.
  5. Bonus tickets. Available only to customers holding at least one purchase ticket. Bonus 1 (WhatsApp message and Instagram follow) and Bonus 2 (Instagram story tagging @ramandurgadasseth) can each be earned once per customer. Referral bonus: one ticket for each referred person who makes a qualifying purchase in the period and names the referrer at billing, up to three per customer. Bonus actions must be shown to RDDS staff at the showroom.
  6. Tickets and register. Each ticket carries a unique printed number. Tickets are placed in a sealed transparent box at the showroom, and each ticket number is recorded against the customer's name and mobile number in the RDDS register. The customer's ticket numbers are noted on their copy of the bill.
  7. Returns. If a bill is cancelled or the jewellery is returned, the tickets for that bill are void. A winning ticket from a returned bill is void and a reserve ticket is used.
  8. The draw. One winning ticket and two reserve tickets will be drawn by hand from all tickets in the box on Sunday 29 November 2026 at 6:00 PM IST at the RDDS showroom, streamed live on Instagram and recorded. The winning number is matched against the register.
  9. Contacting the winner. The winner is called on the registered mobile number during the draw. If the winner cannot be reached within 72 hours, or is not eligible, the first reserve ticket wins, then the second.
  10. Prize. One Apple iPhone 18 Pro, 256GB. Colour subject to availability. The prize is not transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash or jewellery. RDDS may substitute a product of equal or greater value if the model is unavailable.
  11. Tax. The prize is taxable as winnings under the Income-tax Act. Tax deducted at source of 30% plus applicable cess on the prize value is payable by the winner. The winner must pay this amount to RDDS and provide their PAN before the prize is handed over, within 7 days of the draw; otherwise a reserve ticket is used. RDDS will deposit the tax and issue the TDS certificate.
  12. Collection. The prize is handed over in person at the RDDS showroom on showing a valid photo ID.
  13. Publicity. By taking part, the winner agrees that their name, city and photograph may be shared by RDDS on its social media and in-store.
  14. Your information. Details collected are used to run this scheme and, where the customer has agreed, to send WhatsApp updates from RDDS. Reply STOP at any time to stop messages.
  15. Changes. RDDS may amend or withdraw the scheme where required by law or circumstances beyond its control, and will publish any change on this page. RDDS's decision on any matter relating to the scheme is final. Disputes are subject to the jurisdiction of courts in Amritsar.
  16. Platforms. This scheme is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by or associated with Instagram, Meta, WhatsApp or Apple.
